# Revenue Performance Dashboard — Advanced Excel
Managing revenue data scattered across multiple reports often leads to delayed decisions and missed opportunities. This dashboard consolidates all critical business KPIs into one clean, interactive Excel view — giving leadership instant visibility into revenue health across regions, product categories, and time periods.
It tracks ₹10.67M in total revenue across 4 regions, 10+ product categories, and 3 financial years (2022–2024) with quarter-level drill-down — covering Target Attainment, Net Profit, Profit Margin, regional sales, and category-wise profitability trends.

## Objectives
To build a comprehensive, single-view Excel dashboard that enables leadership to monitor revenue performance, track target attainment, analyse regional and category-wise profitability, and identify trends across quarters and years — supporting faster, data-driven business decisions.

## Key Findings

**1. Revenue vs Target Gap:** Total Revenue stands at ₹10.67M against a target of ₹12.55M, with overall target attainment at 85% — indicating a ₹1.88M shortfall requiring strategic review.

**2. Thin Profit Margins:** Net Profit is ₹1.84M on ₹10.67M revenue, resulting in a 17% profit margin — suggesting high operational costs that need closer examination.

**3. West Region Dominates Sales:** West region leads with ₹2.84M in revenue, followed by East (₹2.68M), South (₹2.66M), and North (₹2.49M) — North being the weakest performer and a potential growth opportunity.

**4. Revenue & Profit Move Together:** The Revenue vs Profit Trend chart shows both metrics follow a similar wave pattern across quarters — dipping in Q1 and Q3, peaking around Q2 each year, suggesting seasonal business cycles.

**5. Consistent Category Performance:** Across Electronics and Office categories, revenue and profit margins remain relatively stable — with no single product category significantly outperforming or underperforming others.

**6. Camera & Monitor Lead in Electronics:** Among individual products, Camera and Monitor show slightly higher revenue bars compared to other electronics like Headphones, Keyboard, and Mouse.

**7. Order Volume is Significant:** With 17,261 total orders, the business handles high transaction volume — making automated tracking and dashboard visibility critical for operational efficiency.

**8. Multi-Year Trend Available:** Dashboard covers 2022, 2023, and 2024 with quarter-level drill-down — enabling year-on-year comparison and long-term trend identification.
